# Writing with Blaude

You write; Claude builds the page. Everything is a sentence in chat.

## Publishing

Paste or write your post, then say:

> Turn this into a post.

Claude keeps your words exactly as you wrote them. It asks only for what's missing: a title if there isn't one, and a short description of any image (alt text) for readers who can't see it. The post is published privately and added to your blog as a draft. Open it on claude.ai, choose **Share**, then tell Claude "I've shared it" and it appears on your blog.

## Asking for figures

Say what you want and where. You never need the component names.

- "After the second paragraph, add a step-by-step figure of the three stages."
- "Make the table of results into a bar chart."
- "Put my two photos side by side as a swipeable gallery."
- "Show these commands typing themselves out, with their output."
- "Add a warning box after the setup section: don't skip the backup."
- "Pull out 'A draft is just a post you haven't shared yet' as a quote."
- "Draw a diagram: browser, then server, then database."
- "Animate the request going from the phone to the server and back."

Claude may suggest a figure in one line. It only adds it if you say yes.

## Editing

- "Fix the typo in my last post": only that word changes, and the link stays the same.
- "Add a diagram to the post about caching."
- "Change the title of my last post to …"
- "Add related posts to the caching post": Claude suggests some and waits for your yes.

## Your blog's front page

- "Update my bio." / "Change my intro to …"
- "Add a Talks section" (dated links), "Add a Projects section", "Add a Start here section".
- "Move About to the top." / "Remove the About section."
- "Add my GitHub and my newsletter to the links."
- "Switch to the Featured theme." / "Make my blog look like this" with a screenshot or a description.

## Tips for phone readers

- Put the answer first; the detail can follow.
- Short paragraphs: three or four sentences.
- Headings every few screens, written as statements.
- One or two figures per screen of text at most.

## Limits

Artifacts can't do everything a website can. There's no RSS feed, no comments, no subscribers or view counts, and no custom domain. Videos can't be embedded: Claude adds a thumbnail that links to the video instead. Images are stored inside each post, so a post with many large photos gets heavy. Claude shrinks them, and asks before dropping any.
