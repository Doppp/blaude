How a post gets published

24 Nov 20264 min readguide, basics

You write in Claude the way you already do. Blaude turns that draft into a page with its own link, adds it to your blog, and stays out of the way.

There is no dashboard and no editor to learn. Every step below is a sentence in chat, and the only button you press yourself is Share.

The five steps

Tap through the figure to follow one post from draft to blog.

Your words stay yours. Blaude never rewrites your text. It adds structure, images and figures around it, and asks before changing anything else.

Why the share step is yours

Every page starts private. Claude can build and update your pages, but only you can make them public, from the page itself on claude.ai. That keeps unfinished drafts off the internet by default.

Once a post is public, the next time Claude updates your blog it moves the post from drafts onto your index. Readers who have your blog's link see it straight away.

What gets saved

Your index keeps a small record of every post. You never edit it by hand, but it is what lets Claude find, update and export your posts later.
